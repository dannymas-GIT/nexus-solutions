# NEXUS SOLUTIONS PLATFORM

**When Data Converges, Insight Emerges**

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Nexus Solutions helps established businesses and advisory teams centralize operational data,
define executive KPIs, generate board-ready documents, and deliver client review packages
through a single streamlined workspace.

---

## CORE CAPABILITIES

### Idea Studio & Strategic Planning
- **Structured Brief Intake**: Submit product or initiative concepts with target-market context
- **Milestone Validation & Approval Gates**: Sequential M1/M2 reporting and review workflows
- **Executive Document Generation**: Generate formatted DOCX, PPTX, and PDF plans and summaries

### Analysis Engine & KPI Alignment
- **KPI Definition & Tracking**: Configure leadership metrics across revenue, operations, and delivery
- **Source Planning & Integrations Hub**: Catalog CRM, accounting, spreadsheet, and operational feeds

### Client Deliverable Portal
- **Magic-Link Review**: Share expiring review links with external stakeholders without requiring account setup
- **Preview & Bundle Download**: View interactive static deliverables in-browser or download full ZIP bundles
- **Decision Audit Trail**: Capture client approvals, change requests, and notes in real time

---

## ENGAGEMENT MODEL

| Stage | Focus | Outcome |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Data Convergence Audit** | Map current data sources, spreadsheets, and reporting bottlenecks | Prioritized KPI & automation blueprint |
| **Workspace Setup** | Configure KPIs, reporting templates, and stakeholder review workflows | Repeatable monthly/weekly executive pack |
| **Ongoing Delivery** | Scheduled reporting packs and client portal approvals | Faster leadership decisions with less manual reconciliation |

---

## SECURITY PRACTICES

- HTTPS/TLS transport encryption on production hosts
- API-key authentication for administrative storage, lead, and portal management routes
- Expiring JWT-signed magic links with bounded action counts and path-traversal protection for client deliverables
- Strict CORS origin allowlists and server-side validation
